# Tic-Tac-Toe

Tic-Tac-Toe is a classic game implemented as a web application. Players take turns marking spaces in a 3x3 grid, aiming to get three of their symbols (X or O) in a row, column, or diagonal.

## Features

- **Simple Gameplay:** Easy-to-understand rules suitable for all ages.
- **Player vs Player:** Play against another person locally on the same device.
- **Player vs AI:** Play against an AI opponent with adjustable difficulty levels.
- **Responsive Design:** Enjoy a seamless experience on desktop and mobile devices.

## Usage

1. Open the Tic-Tac-Toe game in your web browser.
2. Choose to play against another person or against the AI.
3. Click on a cell in the grid to place your symbol (X or O).
4. Take turns with your opponent until someone wins or the game ends in a draw.
5. Restart the game to play again or return to the main menu.
